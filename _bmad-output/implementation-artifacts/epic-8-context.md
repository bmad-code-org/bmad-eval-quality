# Epic 8 Context: the shipped ingest, score, and emit stages and their CLI surface

<!-- Compiled from planning artifacts. Edit freely. Regenerate with compile-epic-context if planning docs change. -->

## Goal

Epic 8 finishes v1 by filling in the three stages Epic 7 deliberately left at `module: null` — `ingest`, `score`, and `emit` — and putting a `score` command and one library call on top of them. Every function it composes already exists as a pure, covered reference function; what it adds is the single orchestration path through them, the validation `ingest` owns, the evidence artifact `emit` mints, and the thin adapter AD-14 permits. It closes the remaining half of the spine's owed item 1 by changing `score`'s stage-table row to consume a trial set in the same story that gives `score` a module. When it ends, `scripts/worked-example-target.ts` no longer hand-composes the chain: it calls the shipped stages, which is what turns the worked example from a parallel derivation into evidence.

## Stories

- Story 8.1: The ingest stage and the conditions it records
- Story 8.2: The score stage over a trial set
- Story 8.3: The emit stage and the evidence artifact it mints
- Story 8.4: The score command, the application call, and the published surface
- Story 8.5: The worked chain through the shipped stages, and the release disclosure

## Requirements & Constraints

- `ingest` does not throw on the conditions it detects. AD-16's rule names `core/ingest` as "the enforcement point: an absent, unparseable, incomplete, or violating manifest invalidates the run and records the reason," and AD-24 says ingest "computes its digest from the artifact and invalidates the run when it is absent or incomplete." Invalidation is a rung on AD-21's ladder; a thrown `RuntimeFault` is exit code 5. Two exceptions are named rather than assumed: `schema-parse-failure` is raised at the application boundary before ingest sees anything, and `non-canonicalizable-value` propagates out of `auditQuotation` when a parsed record carries a value `core/canonical` rejects. Ingest itself raises nothing.
- `ingest` receives already-parsed artifacts and never parses. Every shipped `schema-parse-failure` over an artifact is raised in `application/` (`compile.ts:23`, `seal.ts:30`, `preflight.ts:52,65,80,97`, `invoke-port.ts:40`) or `cli/run.ts:164`. The one `core/` raiser is `canonical/scan-json.ts:29,70`, which is the deliberate exception: its header scopes it to JSON syntax and UTF-8 decoding over raw bytes, not to an artifact. No core stage parses an artifact, and `core/stage-contracts.ts` types stages over typed inputs.
- AD-17 binds `ingest` ("judge results arrive inside the sealed run record and are validated on ingest"). Its record-decidable half is `JudgeResult.score === null`; its "the criterion is one the cited rubric declares" half needs the rubric, which no stage row declares, and is recorded as owed rather than assigned.
- No new interchange artifact and no `schemaVersion` bump anywhere in the epic. `validated-observations` and `scored-outcomes-and-verdict` are internal stage products AD-24 exempts from publication, and `stage-table.ts` already carries both with `ownsInterchange: null`.
- No AD-5 code and no AD-28 code is minted. AD-28's ten-code registry already carries `non-canonicalizable-value`, the one fault that reaches a caller through `ingest`, and `schema-parse-failure`, which the application boundary raises first; AD-5 is compile-time only and `score` never runs at compile time.
- AD-14: a command is "one orchestration call plus artifact serialization" and holds no validation, scoring, or policy logic. AD-34: both the library's public surface and the CLI sit on `application/`, so there is one published shape. VFR-8: a capability reachable through one surface and not the other is a defect.
- AD-21's exit codes are PASS/WAIVED/CONCERNS 0, FAIL 2, invalid 3, structural compile failure 4, thrown fault 5. Exit 1 is reachable only through `--strict`, and `--strict` never promotes a CONCERNS whose only firing conditions are the two evidence conditions (`below-minimum-trial-count`, `oracle-unreached`).
- `check:boundary` scans `src/` (through `dist`), `corpus/`, and three `package.json` fields for twelve forbidden strings: `_bmad-output`, `planning-artifact`, `implementation-artifact`, `sprint-status`, `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md`, `bmad`, `TEA`, `stor(y|ies)`, `epics?`, `ACs?[- ]\d`, `Tasks?[- ]\d`, and `Decisions?[- ]\d`. The last one's lookbehind exempts any `ADR-nnn ` prefix, so `ADR-006 Decision 1` passes and a bare `Decision 1` does not. No source comment in this epic may carry any of the twelve.
- `test:coverage` includes `src/core/**` only, at 90% statements and 90% branches. New `src/core/ingest/`, `src/core/score/score.ts`, and `src/core/emit/` code is counted; `src/application/` and `src/cli/` are not.
- `check:ad21-table` imports the ladder graph at load time and forbids a TS enum, namespace, parameter property, or value re-export anywhere in it.
- Ambiguities found mid-story are settled by construction in that story's decisions section rather than escalated into a spine revision or an ADR, as Epic 7 established.

## Technical Decisions

- **AD-24 stage rows are the contract.** `ingest` consumes `sealed-run-record`, `isolation-manifest`, and `evaluator-configuration` with `mode` as its value input, and owns `validated-observations`. `score` consumes `eval-contract`, `validated-observations`, `probe`, `preflight-verdict`, and `scoring-policy`, and owns `scored-outcomes-and-verdict`. `emit` consumes that product and mints `evidence-artifact`. Only `emit` has `lineage: 'mints'`, so only `emit` may write `parentDigest` or `revisionCount`, which is what `check:lineage`'s derived allowlist enforces.
- **Owed item 1's remaining half.** `score`'s declared inputs change to a trial set in the same story that gives `score` a module, per the epic breakdown's own instruction. Story 7.6's `reduceTrialSet` is stage two only; collapsing per-`(probeId, trialIndex)` resolutions into one vote is stage one and is the caller's job, which Story 8.2 is the first code to do.
- **The reference composition already exists.** `scripts/worked-example-target.ts:1071-1381` composes the whole chain: probe sealing, plan indexing, captured-binding resolution, selection, the witness match, per-oracle check resolution, `resolveOutcome`, `reduceTrialSet`, `buildStrengthVector`, coverage evaluation, and the mode's ladder. Story 8.2 lifts that order into `src/` rather than designing a second one. Four `OutcomeInputs` fields the script hardcodes (`waiver`, `judgeConduct`, `evaluationFault`, `probeQualified`) and `preflightPassed` have no derivation in `src/` and must each become a derived value or a documented caller-supplied argument.
- **Three Epic 7 findings fire here.** `reduce-trials.ts:98-105` needs a `default` branch once a caller exists; `reduce-trials.ts:92-95,115` needs a `catchThreshold` range check once a caller exists; `strength.ts:168-177` needs a duplicate-`probeId` guard once outcome arrays are built for real. Stories 8.2 and 8.3 are those moments.
- **The AD-21 table's missing column.** Epic 7's preamble claims the generated ladder table carries an exit code per rung. `LADDER_EXIT_CODES` (`ladder.ts:156-162`) and `LadderResolution.exitCode`/`.strictPromotable` (`:139-146`) exist, but `docs/ad21-verdict-decision.generated.md` renders only `Condition`, `Rung`, `Guard`, and `Evidence condition`. `Evidence condition` is the strict column under another name; the exit-code column is genuinely absent. Story 8.4 adds it.
- **A silent dispatch hole.** `src/cli/run.ts:262-276` dispatches with a two-branch ternary whose `else` runs `seal`, so a new command member typechecks clean and silently seals. Five `Record<Command, …>` literals do fail the compiler (`INPUT_KEYS`, `TAKES_STRICT_INPUTS`, `TAKES_RUN_ID`, `EMITTED`, `COMMAND_USAGE`); `COMMANDS` and the `USAGE` synopsis do not. Story 8.4 closes the hole rather than adding a fourth branch to it.
- **`DiagnosticSink.stage` is a one-member union** (`'preflight'`) at `src/application/diagnostics.ts:13-19`. A score use case that emits diagnostics widens it.

## Checks routed out of Story 8.1, with owners

Three cross-artifact rules name `ingest` in a schema comment and cannot be computed from the three artifacts its stage row declares. Each is routed rather than absorbed, and each is recorded in `deferred-work.md` so it cannot ship unenforced a third time.

- **The `operationId` collision across permitted interfaces** (`sealed-run-record.ts:179-181`) needs `permittedInterfaces`, which lives on the eval contract. Owner: Story 8.2, whose stage row already declares `eval-contract`. `Observation` carries no interface qualifier, so the condition can only name the ambiguous identifier, not the two interfaces.
- **AD-17's rubric-criterion half** (`sealed-run-record.ts:222-227`) needs the rubric to check that a scored criterion is one the cited rubric declares. No stage row declares `rubric` at all; `ARTIFACT_PRODUCERS` gives it to `caller` and nothing consumes it. Recorded as owed with no owner, which is the honest state, rather than assigned to a story that cannot satisfy it.
- **The private-artifact digest recomputation** (`private-artifact-manifest.ts:31-34`) needs bytes resolved through AD-8's corpus port. AD-8's subject is "the core recomputes every per-artifact digest from the resolved bytes" and AD-34 says `application/` "holds no decision logic", so the construction is a pure core comparator called by the application layer that awaited the port. Owner: whichever story first awaits that port, which is not yet an observation. AD-11 makes `ScoringVersionInputs.corpusDigest` (`evidence-artifact.ts:105`) a required recomputation from resolved bytes, and the evidence artifact is `emit`'s, so Story 8.2 or 8.3 may reach the port before Story 8.4 does. Story 8.2 settles the owner in its own decisions and records it here; Story 8.4 is the default if the scoring version turns out to be assembled at the application boundary. `src/ports/corpus-port.ts` and `src/adapters/local-corpus-adapter.ts` both already exist, so this is buildable inside this epic either way.

## Cross-Story Dependencies

- Story 8.2 cannot start before Story 8.1: `score`'s declared input `validated-observations` has no producer until `ingest` has a module, and the conditions Story 8.1 records are the assessment inputs the ladder reads.
- Story 8.2 owes Story 8.1 seven ladder rows. Story 8.1 records a dangling citation from a finding, a dangling citation from an oracle disposition, a record-versus-manifest disagreement, an admitted prohibited input under AD-16's first clause, an absent evaluator configuration, an evaluator configuration whose digest does not recompute, and a malformed judge result, and neither shipped ladder has a row for any of them; AD-32 says its checks invalidate "rather than degrading a verdict", AD-17 says a malformed judge response "invalidates the run rather than becoming a low score", and AD-24 says an absent or incomplete evaluator configuration invalidates the run, so none may be routed to the FAIL row whose guard reads "internally inconsistent under AD-17". Story 8.2 adds the rows, their ladder inputs, their fixtures, and the regenerated table, widens `LadderTarget` to name them, and removes `null` from that union so an eighth rungless condition becomes a compile error. Three of the seven were added during Story 8.1's own code review, which is why the count moved.
- Story 8.3 depends on Story 8.2's outcome and verdict values, and it is the story that makes `strength.ts`'s duplicate-`probeId` guard load-bearing.
- Story 8.4 depends on all three stages existing, because AD-14 forbids the command holding logic of its own; there is nothing to adapt until there is one orchestration call.
- Story 8.5 depends on Stories 8.1 through 8.4 and is the fixed-point proof: the same bytes must come out of the shipped stages that the hand-composition produced, or every difference is named with its cause.
